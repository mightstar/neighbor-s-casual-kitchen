# Note for The AI Firm (under 200 words)

I picked Neighbor's Casual Kitchen in Lake Highlands, Dallas. Their own site is gone. What remains is a stale OpenTable page and a handful of directories. For a neighborhood cafe that lives on brunch, patio tables, and walk-ins, that is a real hole: no way to read the menu, book a table, or order pickup without calling.

The v1 is a finished restaurant site. Hours, address, phone, prices, and dishes come from public listings. The conversion paths are obvious — call, get directions, order pickup, or click a table on a canvas floor plan. Booked tables lock for the chosen period. Sign-in is email and password, nothing more.

If they were paying, I would do three things next: replace Unsplash stand-ins with their own food and room photos; wire a real card-at-pickup or toast-style POS; and add SMS reminders plus a host view so the floor plan is useful on a Saturday night, not just to guests.
